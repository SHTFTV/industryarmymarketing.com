Frequently asked: Drywall & Taping in Vancouver

What does drywall installed cost per square foot in 2026?

$2.20 to $3.10 per sq ft for board plus tape Level-4 finish. Level-5 adds $0.40. Steel stud framing adds $1.10 to $1.60.

What is the difference between Level 4 and Level 5 finish?

Level 4 is paint-ready under matte finishes. Level 5 adds a skim coat across the entire surface — required for satin/semi-gloss and critical lighting conditions.

How long does a 1,500 sq ft drywall job take?

Hang: 2 days. Tape and mud: 5 to 7 days with dry time. Total 8 to 10 working days for a typical condo unit.
