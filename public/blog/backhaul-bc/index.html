Frequently asked: Backhaul & Freight in BC

What is a backhaul and why does it matter?

A backhaul is the return load on an outbound trip. Filling backhauls cuts deadhead miles, drops cost per mile 25 to 40 percent, and improves the carrier P&L by 8 to 12 percent.

How does the exclusive lane work?

One verified carrier per metro pair (Vancouver-Calgary, Vancouver-Edmonton, etc.). Shippers see one trusted carrier, not 40 broker postings.

What NSC rating is required?

BC carriers must hold an NSC certificate. Territory partners maintain Satisfactory rating minimum with current CVOR record.

Do you cover LTL and FTL?

Both. Carrier profiles publish equipment (dry van, reefer, flatbed) and minimum/maximum weight thresholds.

How are loads priced?

Direct rate from carrier — no broker markup. Spot rates publish daily, contract rates available on quarterly agreements.

Why backhaul.io vs DAT or Loadlink?

Load boards are auctions. Backhaul.io is direct relationship with the verified carrier holding the lane.
