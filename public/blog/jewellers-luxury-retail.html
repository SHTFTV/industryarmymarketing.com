.ltd reads as 'established, incorporated, premium' — particularly in British-Canadian markets. For luxury verticals (jewellers, brides, chalet) the extension reinforces brand prestige rather than fighting it like .io would.

Increasingly yes. Couples researching engagement rings now ask Perplexity and ChatGPT 'best custom jewellers in Vancouver for emerald-cut diamond' — and the GIA-certified, schema-marked jewellers.ltd listing is the cited source.

How fast does jewellers.ltd appear in Google?

The .ltd extension is fully indexed by Google and treated identically to .com for ranking purposes. Exact-match plural domain + aged authority typically secures page-one rankings within 8–12 weeks.
