How much does an electrician cost in Birmingham?

Licensed electricians in Birmingham charge $85–$175/hour for residential work. Panel upgrades cost $3,500–$6,500 all-in. EV charger installation runs $900–$2,500. Whole-home rewiring for an older home costs $15,000–$45,000. Permits are required for most electrical work in AL.

Do I need a permit for electrical work in Birmingham?

Yes. In Birmingham, virtually all electrical work beyond replacing fixtures requires a permit from AL's electrical authority. Only licensed electricians can pull electrical permits. Unpermitted work can void home insurance and create liability in a sale.

How much does EV charger installation cost in Birmingham?

A Level 2 EV charger installation in Birmingham costs $900–$2,500 depending on panel capacity and distance. If your panel needs upgrading add $2,000–$4,000. Most installations take 3–5 hours. Several AL utilities offer rebates of $250–$700.

How do I know if my electrical panel needs upgrading in Birmingham?

Signs your Birmingham home needs a panel upgrade: breakers trip frequently, you have a 60-amp or 100-amp fused panel, you're adding an EV charger or hot tub, or your insurance company is flagging the panel. A licensed electrician can assess your panel in 30–45 minutes.

What electrical work can I do myself in Birmingham?

In Birmingham, homeowners can legally replace fixtures, outlets, and switches on existing circuits. Any work involving the panel, new circuits, or wiring behind walls requires a licensed electrician and permit. DIY electrical work on unpermitted systems can void home insurance.
