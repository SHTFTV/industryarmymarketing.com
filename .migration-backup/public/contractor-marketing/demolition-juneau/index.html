Do I need a permit to demolish a building in Juneau?

Juneau requires a demolition permit from the municipal building department for any structure removal. The process includes a hazardous materials assessment for pre-1990 buildings, utility disconnection confirmation, and neighbour notification. Permit timelines typically run 2–6 weeks.
