Do I need a permit for electrical work in Brisbane?

Yes. In Brisbane, virtually all electrical work beyond replacing fixtures requires a permit from AU's electrical authority. Only licensed electricians can pull electrical permits. Unpermitted work can void home insurance and create liability in a sale.

What electrical work can I do myself in Brisbane?

In Brisbane, homeowners can legally replace fixtures, outlets, and switches on existing circuits. Any work involving the panel, new circuits, or wiring behind walls requires a licensed electrician and permit. DIY electrical work on unpermitted systems can void home insurance.
