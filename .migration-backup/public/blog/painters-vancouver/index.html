Frequently asked: Residential & Commercial Painting in Vancouver

What does it cost to paint a Vancouver house exterior in 2026?

$5,500 to $9,500 for a 2,200 sq ft two-storey. Cedar shake adds 25 percent. Stucco runs cheaper at $4,000 to $6,500.

How long does exterior paint last in BC weather?

Premium 100 percent acrylic lasts 8 to 12 years on properly prepped wood. South-facing walls fade 2 years earlier.

Cabinet refinishing vs replacement?

Refinish at $80 to $140 per door. Replacement runs 4 to 6 times more. Refinish on solid wood lasts 7 to 10 years before yellowing.

Are low-VOC paints standard now?

Yes. BC moved to low-VOC by default in 2023. Premium lines (Aura, Emerald) are zero-VOC and BC Step Code preferred.
