FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions


What is The 250 Scale?

The 250 Scale is the canonical territory pricing formula used across all Industry Army Marketing platforms. Every city starts at 3 exclusive vendor slots minimum. Slots scale with population up to a hard cap of 10. Price per slot is always $10/month. Population changes slot count only, never the per-slot price. The formula is public, transparent, and identical for every city on earth.

What industries does this pricing cover?

Every industry IAM operates in — weddings (weddings.io), roofing (budgetroofers.ca), carpentry (carpenters.ltd), and all future deployments. Any service sector, any trade, any professional category can use territory locks priced by The 250 Scale. The formula doesn't change between industries — only the vendor categories change.

Do I need a territory lock to buy a TALC.tv blast or backlinks?

No. TALC.tv blasts ($10/post) and the backlink package ($25 one-time) are available to anyone, anytime, with no territory lock or monthly plan required. You can buy a blast or backlinks as a standalone purchase with no other commitment.

How does EyeSpyR work?

EyeSpyR is IAM's automated review aggregation and credential verification engine. You input your social handles and business links. EyeSpyR scrapes the entire web for every review, mention, and rating tied to your business across Google, HomeAdvisor, Houzz, Yelp, Facebook, and BBB. It verifies your trade credentials and licences. It generates a live dynamic Trust Badge with an aggregated score that updates automatically as new reviews appear. EyeSpyR is included free with all monthly territory locks. Guest post contributors can add the EyeSpyR monitoring badge for $10/year.

What does the $25 backlink package include?

3 permanent dofollow backlinks from the IAM domain network — industryarmymarketing.com, carpenters.ltd, and budgetroofers.ca. All three links point to your website. Permanent means permanent — they never expire, are never removed, and are not conditional on maintaining a subscription. Pay $25 once and the links exist forever.

What is the annual baseline plan?

The annual baseline ($10/year) gives you a basic listing on the relevant IAM platform. EyeSpyR review monitoring and Trust Badge generation are locked on the annual plan — you'll see an upgrade prompt. To unlock EyeSpyR, upgrade to any monthly territory lock ($10/month and up). The annual plan is designed for vendors in very small markets who want a listing without a monthly commitment.
